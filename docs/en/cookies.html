IN SHORT

In short: this website does not track you and does not use cookies for analytics or advertising. That is why we do not show a cookie consent banner.

What we store on your device

We store only two pieces of information, and only when you choose something yourself:

NAME
br-theme
TYPE
Browser local storage (localStorage), not a cookie
PURPOSE
Remembers whether you chose light or dark mode
WHEN
Only after you click the mode switch
HOW LONG
Until you delete this website’s data in your browser
NAME
br-lang
TYPE
Browser local storage (localStorage), not a cookie
PURPOSE
Remembers the language you chose for the website (Polish or English)
WHEN
Only after you choose a language
HOW LONG
Until you delete this website’s data in your browser

This information stays in your browser — we do not send it to ourselves or to anyone else.

Third-party services

Directions map

The Google map loads only when you click “Show map”. From that moment Google may store its own cookies, in line with its privacy policy.

Online booking

The booking form runs on a separate service, online.beefandreef.pl, which has its own cookie rules.

Fonts

We load the typeface from our own server, not from Google Fonts, so your IP address is not sent to Google when the page is displayed.

How to delete stored data

In your browser settings, delete the cookies and data for this website. The website will then return to the mode that matches your device settings.

More information

We describe how we process booking and enquiry data in the privacy policy. Questions? Write to kontakt@beefandreef.pl.
