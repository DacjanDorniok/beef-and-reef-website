About us

A place born of a passion for top-quality steaks and fresh seafood.

We combine classic culinary techniques with a modern approach, creating dishes with bold flavour, perfect texture and exceptional presentation. Here fine cooking meets an elegant atmosphere and professional service — the ideal setting for a special dinner, a business meeting or celebrating the moments that matter.

Head chef

Klaudiusz Danowski

At the heart of our kitchen is head chef Klaudiusz Danowski, who composes every dish with remarkable attention to detail, using only carefully selected produce.
