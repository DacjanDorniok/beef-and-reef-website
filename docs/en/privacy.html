We respect your privacy. Below we explain what data we collect when you book a table or ask about a party, why we collect it and what rights you have.

1. Data controller

The controller of your personal data is BEEF AND REEF spółka z ograniczoną odpowiedzialnością (a limited liability company) with its registered office at ul. Krzysztofa Kieślowskiego 7/38, 02-961 Warszawa, Poland, entered in the National Court Register (KRS) under no. 0001222898, NIP (tax ID) 9512640807, REGON 543961779, which runs the restaurant at ul. Kazachska 1, 02-999 Warszawa — hereinafter “Beef & Reef”.

For any matters concerning your personal data, write to kontakt@beefandreef.pl or call +48 222 538 860.

2. What data we collect

We only collect the data you give us yourself:

  • when you book a table — your name, phone number, possibly your e-mail address, the date and the number of guests;
  • when you ask about a party — your contact details and the information about the event that you describe in a conversation or message;
  • when a party is paid by invoice — the details needed to issue it.

Providing your data is voluntary, but without it we cannot accept a booking or reply to an enquiry.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Handling bookings and organising parties — Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps taken before entering into a contract, and its performance).
  • Replying to messages and contacting you about your visit — Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest).
  • Issuing invoices and keeping accounts — Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR (legal obligation).
  • Establishing, pursuing or defending possible claims — Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

4. Online booking

Online booking takes place in an external booking system at online.beefandreef.pl, operated by Choice (choiceqr.com). Data from the form goes to that system and is processed on our behalf under a data processing agreement.

5. Who we share data with

Your data may only be received by companies that help us run the restaurant — to the extent necessary for their tasks:

  • the online booking system provider,
  • the website and e-mail hosting provider,
  • our accounting office — in the case of invoices.

We do not sell your data or share it for other companies’ marketing purposes.

6. How long we keep data

  • Booking data — until your visit, and then until any possible claims become time-barred.
  • Correspondence about parties — until the matter is closed, and then until any possible claims become time-barred.
  • Accounting documents — for the period required by tax law.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it rectified, erased, restricted and transferred, and to object to processing based on our legitimate interest.

To exercise these rights, write to kontakt@beefandreef.pl. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

8. Cookies and social media

This website does not use cookies for analytics or advertising. You will find the details in the cookie information.

The links to Facebook and Instagram lead to external services — we do not embed their plugins on the website. Once you go there, those services’ privacy policies apply.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change the way we process data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
